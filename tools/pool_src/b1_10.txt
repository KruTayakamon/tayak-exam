R three-opinions 2|Should Shops Charge for Plastic Bags?
Malee, 34: I think it's a good idea. Since my local supermarket started charging one baht per bag, I always bring my own cloth bag. If it costs money, people remember.

Tom, 52: I don't agree. One baht is too little to change anything. The government should ban plastic bags completely, especially in markets, where most of them are used.

Arisa, 21: I understand both sides. Charging is a start, but shops should also give people cheap reusable bags. Many older people don't know where to buy them.
Q What does Malee do now?
=she brings a cloth bag|I always bring my own cloth bag
-she pays for plastic bags|เธอบอกว่าตอนนี้พาถุงผ้าไปเอง
-she shops at the market|ไม่ได้พูดถึงตลาด
-she sells cloth bags|ไม่ได้พูดว่าขายถุง
Q What is Tom's opinion?
=plastic bags should be banned|should ban plastic bags completely
-one baht is a fair price|เขาบอกว่าหนึ่งบาทน้อยเกินไป
-markets should charge more|เขาอยากให้ห้าม ไม่ใช่ขึ้นราคา
-the idea is good|เขาไม่เห็นด้วย
Q What does Arisa suggest?
=selling cheap reusable bags|shops should also give people cheap reusable bags
-stopping the charge|เธอว่าการเก็บเงินเป็นจุดเริ่ม
-teaching young people|เธอพูดถึงผู้สูงอายุ
-closing some markets|ไม่ได้พูดถึงการปิดตลาด
