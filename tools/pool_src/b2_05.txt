R reflection-article 2|What Our Failed Market Taught Us
Two years ago, our community group opened a Sunday night market in the village square. We expected large crowds, but after four months we had to close it.

Looking back, the problems were obvious. We chose Sunday night because it was convenient for us, but most visitors had to work early on Monday. We also neglected advertising, believing that people would simply hear about it. If we had asked local people what they wanted, we would have chosen a different day.

We don't regret trying. The experience taught us to listen before planning. This year we reopened the market on Saturday afternoons after a survey of 300 residents, and it has been busy every week.
Q Why did the Sunday market fail?
