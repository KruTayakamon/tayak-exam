R activity-schedule 2|Riverside Community Centre: December Activities
Here is our programme for December. All activities are free for local residents, but you need to book a place at the front desk.

Yoga classes take place every Monday and Wednesday at 6 pm. Please bring your own mat. The English conversation club meets twice a month, on the first and third Saturday, from 10 to 11.30 am.

The centre will be closed from 24 to 26 December. On 31 December, there is a New Year party in the garden from 7 pm. Families are welcome, and there will be music and food from local shops.
