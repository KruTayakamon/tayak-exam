R workplace-advice 2|Five Ways to Make Meetings Shorter
Many workers say they spend too much time in meetings that achieve very little. Here are some simple ways to improve them.

First, send the agenda at least a day before, so everyone can come prepared. Second, invite only the people who really need to be there; others can read the notes later. Third, start on time, even if someone is late, to reinforce the idea that time matters.

Fourth, end each point with a clear action and the name of the person responsible. Finally, try standing meetings for short updates. People naturally keep them brief when they are not sitting comfortably.
Q Why should the agenda be sent early?
=so people can prepare|so everyone can come prepared
-so people can decline|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
-so the meeting can start late|ควรเริ่มตรงเวลา
-so the notes are shorter|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
Q What should people who don't need to attend do?
=read the notes afterwards|others can read the notes later
-join online|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
-send a replacement|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
-arrive late|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
Q Why are standing meetings suggested?
=people tend to keep them short|People naturally keep them brief
-there are not enough chairs|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
-they are healthier|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
-managers prefer them|ไม่ได้กล่าวถึง
