R research-abstract 2|Abstract (Fictional): Teacher Attrition in Rural Thailand
This study examines the causes of teacher attrition in 42 rural schools between 2018 and 2023. Data were drawn from employment records and interviews with 120 current and former teachers.

The findings indicate that attrition was highest in the first three years of service, with nearly one in four new teachers leaving their post. Isolation and limited professional development emerged as more significant factors than salary. Teachers who had a mentor during their first year were considerably less likely to leave.

The study concludes that mentoring should be treated not as an optional extra but as a prerequisite for sustainable staffing. Further research is needed to establish whether online mentoring can achieve similar results in very remote areas.
