R opinion-article 2|Should Homework Be Banned in Primary Schools?
Some parents argue that homework puts too much pressure on young children. Families are busy, and children need time to play and rest. Moreover, studies suggest that homework has little effect on learning before the age of ten.

On the other hand, many teachers believe that short tasks help children develop good habits. Reading for fifteen minutes at home, for example, can be beneficial, especially for children who don't have books at school. Banning homework completely might also send the message that learning only happens in the classroom.

Perhaps the answer is not a ban but a limit. Short, optional activities that families can do together would arguably keep the benefits while reducing stress.
