R opinion-column 2|Column: Stop Telling Me to "Just Relax"
Every January, I am told that the secret to a better life is to relax more. Magazines suggest yoga, candles and "digital detox" weekends. All very nice, I'm sure, if you have the time and money.

But for many working parents I know, the problem isn't that we don't know how to relax. It's that we have no time to do it. Telling an exhausted nurse on a double shift to "practise mindfulness" is, frankly, a little condescending.

What would genuinely help? Shorter commutes, affordable childcare and employers who don't send emails at midnight. I am not cynical about wellbeing; I simply think it is about time we stopped treating a social problem as a personal failing.
