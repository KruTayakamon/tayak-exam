R first-year-story 2|My First Year as a Hospital Pharmacist
When I started at the hospital, I had just finished university and had never worked night shifts. For the first two months, I was constantly tired, and I would often make small mistakes, such as putting medicine in the wrong drawer.

My supervisor, Khun Duangjai, noticed that I was struggling. Instead of criticising me, she suggested that I write a checklist for every task. Initially I was reluctant, because I thought checklists were for beginners. But I had to admit that I was a beginner.

Gradually, things improved. By the end of the year, I had stopped making the same mistakes, and I had started training new staff myself. Looking back, the biggest challenge wasn't the work itself. It was accepting that I needed help.
Q What problem did the writer have at the beginning?
=making small mistakes because of tiredness|constantly tired ... make small mistakes
-arguing with the supervisor|หัวหน้าช่วยเหลือ ไม่ได้ทะเลาะกัน
-not finishing university|เขาเพิ่งเรียนจบ
-being given too few tasks|ไม่ได้พูดว่างานน้อย
Q Why was the writer reluctant to use a checklist at first?
=he felt it was for inexperienced staff|I thought checklists were for beginners
-it took too much time|ไม่ได้พูดว่าเสียเวลา
-his supervisor didn't use one|ไม่ได้พูดถึงหัวหน้าใช้หรือไม่
-he had too many tasks to list|ไม่ได้พูดถึงเรื่องนี้
Q According to the writer, what was hardest?

-the night shifts|เป็นปัญหาช่วงแรก แต่ไม่ใช่ที่ยากที่สุด
