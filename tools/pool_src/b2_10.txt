R research-summary 2|Summary: Phone Use Before Sleep (Small Classroom Study)
This small study looked at 40 adult learners in an evening English class. Each person kept a diary for two weeks, recording how long they used their phone before sleeping and how rested they felt the next morning.

The results appear to suggest a link between phone use and sleep. Learners who used their phones for more than an hour before bed reported feeling tired on 9 out of 14 mornings, compared with 4 out of 14 for those who used them for less than 15 minutes.

However, the study has clear limits. The group was small, and the diaries depended on people's own reports. The findings should therefore be seen as tentative. A larger study with sleep monitors would be needed to confirm them.
