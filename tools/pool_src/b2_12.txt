R professional-development 2|Learning After Forty
When Khun Arunee turned forty-two, she decided to learn computer programming. She had worked as an accountant for twenty years and wanted a new challenge.

"At first I felt embarrassed," she says. "Most people in my online class were in their twenties." But she found that her experience helped her. She already knew how businesses worked, so she could understand why certain programs were needed.

Arunee studied for an hour every morning before work. After eighteen months, she built a simple app that helps small shops keep track of their accounts. She now works part-time for a software company and still does some accounting. "I didn't change my career completely," she explains. "I added a new skill to it."
