I bought this for my family of five. It cooks rice perfectly and keeps it warm for hours. It has a timer, so I can set it at night and have rice ready in the morning. The only disadvantage is the size: it takes up a lot of space in my small kitchen.

This is perfect for one or two people. It's light and easy to clean, and it cooks rice in just 20 minutes. However, it doesn't keep rice warm for long, and there's no timer. For the price, it's very good value.
