R formal-correspondence 2|Letter: Response to the Proposed Merger
Dear Dr Wanchai,

Thank you for your letter of 3 May outlining the proposed merger of our two departments. I concur that closer cooperation would benefit our students, and I appreciate the care with which the plan has been prepared.

I must, however, respectfully demur on one point. The proposal suggests that the combined department would be led from your campus. With deference to your experience, I am not entirely convinced that this arrangement would serve students on our campus, who would lose access to senior staff.

Might I suggest that leadership be shared for an initial period of two years? I would be glad to discuss this further at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,
Dr Rattana Phuong
