The results indicate that attendance increased by 9 per cent on average. Children were found to be more active in afternoon lessons, although this was based mainly on teachers' opinions rather than test scores.

The main weakness identified was cost. Food prices rose during the year, and two schools had to reduce portion sizes in the final term. The team recommends that the budget should be reviewed every six months.
