R incident-report 2|Incident Report: Lift Stuck on Floor 4
On Tuesday at about 2.15 pm, the main lift stopped between the third and fourth floors. Two staff members and a visitor were inside. They were travelling to a meeting on the fifth floor when the lights flickered and the lift suddenly stopped.

One of the staff members pressed the alarm button immediately, and security called the lift company. While they were waiting, the security guard talked to them through the intercom to keep them calm.

The engineer arrived at 3 pm and opened the doors ten minutes later. Nobody was hurt, but the visitor said she felt a little confused and tired. The lift will be checked again on Friday.
