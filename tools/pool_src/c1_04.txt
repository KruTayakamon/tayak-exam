R formal-regulation 2|Notice: Use of Personal Devices (Fictional Agency)
With effect from 1 March, the following conditions apply to the use of personal devices at all branch offices.

Staff may connect personal laptops to the guest network only. Connection to the internal network is prohibited unless written approval has been obtained from the IT department. Any device found to be connected without approval will be disconnected and the matter recorded.

The onus is on each employee to ensure that their device has up-to-date security software. Notwithstanding the above, branch managers may, at their discretion, permit temporary access in an emergency, provided that the IT department is informed within 24 hours.

-the IT department|ไอทีเป็นผู้อนุมัติ ไม่ได้รับผิดชอบซอฟต์แวร์ส่วนตัว
