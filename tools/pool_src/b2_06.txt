R letter-to-editor 2|Letter to the Editor: Bus Lanes Are Not the Problem
Dear Editor,

Your article last week suggested that the new bus lanes on Charoen Road have made traffic worse. I disagree. The article implied that drivers are the only people who use the road, but more than 3,000 people take the bus along that route every day.

Apparently, many drivers assume the bus lanes are empty most of the time. In fact, buses run every six minutes at rush hour. I take the number 18 to work, and my journey has become fifteen minutes shorter since the lanes opened.

I accept that the changes should have been explained better before they began. But I hope your newspaper will speak to bus passengers before writing about this again.

Somjit P.
