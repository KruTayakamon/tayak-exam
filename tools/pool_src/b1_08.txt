R saving-money 2|Small Steps to Save Money
Many people think saving money is only possible with a high salary. In fact, small changes can make a big difference.

First, write down everything you spend for one month. Most people are surprised by how much they spend on snacks and coffee. If you made coffee at home instead of buying it, you could save more than 1,000 baht a month.

Second, try the "48-hour rule". If you want to buy something that isn't essential, wait two days. Often you will realise that you don't really need it.

Finally, put some money into a savings account as soon as you get paid, not at the end of the month. This way, you won't spend it by mistake.

=small changes can help you save|small changes can make a big difference
-you need a high salary to save|บทความบอกว่าไม่จริง
-coffee is bad for your health|ไม่ได้พูดเรื่องสุขภาพ
-banks give the best advice|ไม่ได้พูดถึงคำแนะนำจากธนาคาร
Q What is the "48-hour rule"?

-spend only 48 baht a day|ไม่ได้พูดถึงตัวเลขนี้
-shop only twice a week|ไม่ได้พูดถึงความถี่การซื้อ
