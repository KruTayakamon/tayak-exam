R editorial 2|Editorial: A Pivotal Moment for Our Rivers
Rarely has a single report galvanised public opinion so quickly. Last week's study, showing plastic levels in our three main rivers had doubled in five years, has become the talk of every market and office.

What the report underscores is not merely the scale of the problem but its speed. Five years is less than the time it takes most children to finish primary school. Should the trend continue, fishing communities downstream could lose their livelihoods within a decade.

This is a pivotal moment. Sweeping bans on single-use plastics are not the only answer, but they are a necessary start. What is needed now is not another committee but action: deposit schemes for bottles, support for refill shops and, above all, enforcement of the rules we already have.
