R holiday-postcard 1|A Postcard from Phuket
Dear Joy,
We are in Phuket! We arrived on Monday after a long drive. The hotel is near the beach, and our room is very nice.
On Tuesday, the weather was beautiful, so we visited three small islands by boat. On Wednesday it rained all day, so we stayed at the hotel and played cards. Yesterday we walked to the old town and tried some delicious food.
We go home on Saturday.
Love,
Nida
